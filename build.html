I'm the cofounder of PlanArc, a student success startup backed by Ann Arbor SPARK and UM Innovation Partnerships. PlanArc helps students with executive functioning challenges turn intentions into action. We completed user testing with UM students in Spring 2026 and are piloting with 750 students at the University of Michigan this fall.

I mentor UMSI masters students as they extend course projects into research. I've coauthored 3 papers with masters students since arriving at UMSI. Current student-directed projects include virtual cell and tissue simulations, agent-based models of housing market dynamics, electoral modeling, workforce development, and AI-assisted scientific workflows for computational biology.
